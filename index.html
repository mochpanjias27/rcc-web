          About Research Collaboration Community

          Research Collaboration Community (Rescollacomm) was founded in Bandung on 25 May 2019 to advance quantitative research and mathematical modeling in Indonesia. Today it manages ten international journals across three publishing units, RCC, IORA, and CRP, runs writing and publication workshops with universities across the country, and offers tutoring for school students.
