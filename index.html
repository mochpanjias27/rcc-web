        About Research Collaboration Community

        Research Collaboration Community (Rescollacomm) was founded in Bandung on 25 May 2019 to advance quantitative research and mathematical modeling in Indonesia. Today it manages ten international journals across three publishing units, RCC, IORA, and CRP, and runs writing workshops with universities across the country.
