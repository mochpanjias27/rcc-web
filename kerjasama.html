Partnership packages

Start with a single activity or commit to a longer partnership. Every package is tailored, and pricing follows the scope we agree with you.

01

For faculties and research units

Workshop and coaching clinic for Scopus publication

A hands-on program that takes lecturers from a draft to a manuscript ready to submit to a Scopus-indexed journal.

What is included

  • Talks on choosing the right journal and avoiding predatory ones
  • Manuscript structure, writing, and responding to reviewers
  • Coaching clinic on participants' own drafts, in small groups
  • Trainers with active Scopus publication records
  • Certificates for participants
Format
1 to 2 days, on campus or online
Outputs
Manuscripts reviewed by trainers, a list of target journals for each participant, and an activity report for your institution
Request a proposal

02

For universities and associations

Co-hosted seminars and international conferences with publication outputs

RCC partners with your institution to run an international seminar or conference, and handles the publication side so presenters leave with published papers.

What is included

  • Planning of theme, scope, and schedule together
  • Call for papers, submission, and peer review management
  • Publication in the ORICS proceedings or RCC's international journals, with Crossref DOI
  • Keynote and invited speakers from the RCC network
  • Support for promotion through RCC channels
Format
Hybrid, on campus, or online
Outputs
Published proceedings or journal articles, a conference report, and documentation
Request a proposal

03

For long-term partners

MoU and MoA partnership

A formal, multi-year partnership that brings training, publication support, and joint academic activities into one agreement.

What is included

  • A yearly program of workshops and webinars for lecturers and students
  • Lecturers from your institution invited as reviewers or editorial board members of RCC journals, based on expertise
  • Joint research and community service activities
  • Co-hosted seminars and conferences
  • A named contact person at RCC for your institution
Format
MoU for the overall partnership, followed by an MoA for each activity
Outputs
A signed agreement, a yearly activity plan, and reports for accreditation or institutional performance indicators

Our publication policy. Every paper, including those from partner events, goes through independent peer review. We do not guarantee acceptance, and partnerships never buy publication.

How a partnership works

  1. Initial discussion

    Tell us your goals, participants, timeline, and budget, on WhatsApp or in a short meeting.

  2. Proposal

    We send a proposal with the agenda, trainers or speakers, outputs, and costs.

  3. Agreement

    We formalise it with an MoU, MoA, or work agreement that fits your institution's procedures.

  4. Delivery and report

    We run the activity and send a report with documentation for your records and accreditation.

Questions from institutions

Can a partnership guarantee that our lecturers' papers are published?

No. Every paper goes through peer review, and editorial decisions are independent of any partnership. What we do is help authors prepare stronger manuscripts and choose suitable journals.

How much does a program cost?

It depends on the duration, number of participants, format, and location. We send a detailed quotation with the proposal, after the initial discussion.

Can the workshop be held at our campus outside Bandung?

Yes. We have run programs in Bogor and Sukabumi, and online sessions can reach any location.

What documents do you provide for our records?

Depending on the package: the MoU or MoA, an activity report with documentation, attendance lists, and certificates for participants.

Can we start with one activity before signing an MoU?

Yes. Many partners begin with a single workshop, then formalise a longer partnership once they have seen the results.
