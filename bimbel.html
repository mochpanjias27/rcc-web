    Frequently asked questions

    How do I sign up?

    Choose a program above and tap "Sign up on WhatsApp", or use the consultation form at the top of the page. The admin will reply and help you through the rest.

    How much does tutoring cost?

    Each program shows a price range per session, from IDR 50,000 for elementary school. The admin confirms the exact price for your program before the first lesson.

    Which subjects are available?

    English, mathematics, and science for SD, SMP, and SMA, with physics, chemistry, and biology for SMA. We also prepare students for UTBK-SNBT and applicants for the CPNS basic competency test (TWK, TIU, TKP).

    When are lessons held?

    The schedule is agreed with the admin when you sign up, based on the student's availability.

    Can I ask before deciding?

    Yes. The consultation is free, and there is no obligation to sign up.
