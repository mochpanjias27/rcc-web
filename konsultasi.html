Three consulting services

Choose one service or combine them. Every engagement starts with a short discussion of your project, your field and your deadline.

01

Data analysis consulting

Konsultasi pengolahan data

Statistics for your research, from choosing the method to explaining the results, using SPSS, structural equation modelling (SEM) and other statistical tools.

  • Choosing the right method for your research question and data
  • Data preparation, validity and reliability tests
  • Analysis in SPSS, SEM and other statistical software
  • Reading the output and writing up the results

02

Research article and SLR writing consulting

Konsultasi penulisan artikel riset dan SLR

Guidance through every stage of developing a research article or a systematic literature review, with support through peer review until acceptance in a reputable journal.

  • Developing the idea, structure and argument of the article
  • Choosing a suitable reputable journal and avoiding predatory ones
  • Feedback on drafts before submission
  • Support in responding to reviewers until the article is accepted

03

SLR consulting for articles and final theses

Konsultasi SLR untuk artikel dan tugas akhir

A rigorous systematic literature review, from the search strategy to a clear novelty claim, for a journal article or for a thesis or dissertation.

  • Search strategy and screening following the PRISMA flow
  • Bibliometric analysis and mapping of the literature
  • Identifying the research gap and stating the novelty claim
  • Structuring the review chapter or article

We work with you, not for you

Consulting is guidance. Your research stays yours, and so does the authorship.

  • You remain the author. We explain, review and advise; you write and decide.
  • Your data stays honest. We analyse the data you collected and never alter or invent results.
  • Editors decide. We help you prepare a stronger manuscript, but acceptance is always the journal's independent decision.
  • Your work stays confidential. Drafts and data are used only for your consultation.

Frequently asked questions

Can you guarantee that my article will be accepted?

No one can honestly guarantee acceptance, because every article goes through independent peer review. We support you through each revision until the article is accepted, and help you choose journals where your work has a realistic chance.

Will you write the article or thesis for me?

No. We guide, explain and review, but the writing and the decisions are yours. This keeps your work original and makes sure you can defend it in front of reviewers or examiners.

How much does a consultation cost?

It depends on the service, the scope and the deadline. After a short discussion we send a clear quotation before any work begins.

Which software do you work with?

SPSS and SEM software for data analysis, and bibliometric tools for literature mapping. Tell us what you already use and we will work with it where possible.

Can consultations be held online?

Yes. Sessions can be online from anywhere, or in person at the RCC office in Riung Bandung.
