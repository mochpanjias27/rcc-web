What we can run for you

Every program is tailored to the participants, their fields, and the outcome your institution needs.

Our core program

Scopus publication workshop and coaching clinic

For lecturers and researchers preparing an article for a Scopus-indexed journal, with time to work on their own manuscripts alongside the trainers.

  • Finding the right Scopus journal and avoiding predatory ones
  • Structuring the manuscript: title, abstract, methods, and discussion
  • Responding to reviewers and revising
  • One-on-one coaching on participants' drafts

Scientific writing workshop

From research idea to a well-structured article, for lecturers, postgraduate students, and early-career researchers.

Research data and AI tools

Practical sessions on analysing data with SPSS and using AI tools responsibly for literature review and writing.

Seminars and conferences with published outputs

Planning an international seminar or conference? RCC can take care of the publication side, so presenters leave with a published paper.

  1. Plan the event together

    Theme, scope, schedule, and the publication venues that fit the papers.

  2. Call for papers

    We help publicise the call and manage submissions.

  3. Peer review

    Papers are reviewed by editors and reviewers from RCC's journal network.

  4. Publication

    Accepted papers are published in the ORICS proceedings or RCC's international journals, each with a Crossref DOI.
